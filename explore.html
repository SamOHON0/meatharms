Explore Aughrim.

A valley village in the Wicklow hills, twice named Ireland's Tidiest Small Town.

Around the village.

Aughrim won Ireland's Tidiest Small Town in 2006 and 2009, and was part of Ireland's Entente Florale success in 2007.

The Wicklow Way

Aughrim is the nearest town to the Iron Bridge stage of the Wicklow Way, about 9km from the trail. We arrange taxis and luggage transfers for walkers staying with us.

Riverside woodland walk

A 4km walk follows the water from the village into Tinnakilly Woods, under alder, oak, birch and sycamore.

Fishing

There's a stocked angling lake at the village. Ask us when you book and we'll point you right.

Golf at Woodenbridge

Woodenbridge Golf Club is a short drive down the valley towards Avoca.

Avoca & the Meeting of the Waters

The Vale of Avoca, Avoca village and the Meeting of the Waters are a short drive from the inn.

Glendalough & the coast

The monastic site and lakes at Glendalough, and the sand at Brittas Bay, are both within an easy drive.

Getting here.

Aughrim is about an hour and a quarter south of Dublin.

Take the N11 south to Rathnew, then follow the signs for Rathdrum and on to Aughrim. The inn is on Main Street.
