One of Wicklow's oldest inns.

The Meath Arms has been on Main Street in Aughrim since 1728 and is run today by the Phelan family.

There are ten en suite bedrooms upstairs and a bar downstairs with live music at the weekends. The village sits in a valley in the Wicklow hills, with walking, golf and fishing nearby.
