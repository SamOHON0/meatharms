The Meath Arms is one of Wicklow's oldest inns, run today by the Phelan family. It's the kind of place where the fire is lit, the pint is good and nobody's a stranger for long.

Whether you're walking the hills, touring the Garden County or just passing through Aughrim, there's a seat at the bar and a bed upstairs.
