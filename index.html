The heart of the village since 1728.

The Meath Arms is one of Wicklow's oldest inns, run today by the Phelan family. The fire is lit, the pint is good and nobody stays a stranger for long.

Walkers come down off the hills, tourers stop on their way through the Garden County, and the locals hold up their end of the bar. There has been a welcome waiting here for nearly three hundred years.

Made for walkers, golfers and wanderers.

Aughrim sits in a green valley in the Wicklow hills, a national Tidy Towns winner about an hour and a quarter south of Dublin.

The Wicklow trails, golf and river fishing are all on the doorstep. We look after walkers with taxis and luggage transfers, and after a day outside there is a warm bar and a comfortable bed waiting.
